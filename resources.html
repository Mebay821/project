Water Conservation Resources

Practical managment advice for residential standby intervals

1. Storage Container Care

Clean and sanitize your water barrels throughly using food-grade methodsprior to distribution cycles. Store containers away from direct sunlight to suppressorganic growth.

2. Greywater Re-use

Repurpose non-potable drainge water from clothes washing and kitchen rinsing activities to manage household sanitization, floor cleaning, and garden maintainance requirments.

3. Consumption Auditing

Track active residential use habits by checking for minor leaks around internal plumbing fittings and prioritizing primary supply reserves strictly for critical nutrition needs.



SAFETY ADVISORY:Ensure all secondary reserve tanks and storage drums remain securely sealed to prevent domestic biologicalcontamination during extended pipeline off-cycles.
