Escape the ordinary

My late-night sanctuary

There is a specific kind of that only exsits past midnight, when the rest of the world falls silent. wrapped in a cozy blanket with a warm cup of spiced tea, my mind drifts away from reality and staraight into kingdoms built on high stakes, soaring dragons, and heart-wrenching sacrifices. This corner is dedicated to the stories that keep me awake , questioning everything, and feeling entirely alive.

Current literary obsessions

  • Throne of glass: An epic journey of a brilliant assassin reclaiming a broken crown.it is a master class in world-building, devastating loyalty, and fighting through the ashes.
  • Fourth wing:where deadly dragon-rider trials meet scroching tension. You can practically hear the thunder of wings and smell the ozone in the air with every page turn
  • Once upon a broken heart: A whimsical, pastel-ghotic fairytale dripping with sweet curses, deceptive Jacks, and the dangerous consequences of making deals with the fates.

Cinematic Masterpieces & TV Shows

  1. Attack on Titan: Far more than just an anime. It is a brutal, philosophical epic about freedom, cyclical hatred, and the tragic burdens of a gray morality world. The political intrigue perfectly mirrorshigh-fantasy court betrayal.
  2. The Witcher: A dark fantasy series that follows the journey of Geralt of Rivia, a monster hunter navigating a world of magic, politics, and moral ambiguity.
