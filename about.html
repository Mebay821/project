About this Project

This project aims to provide a simple solution for managing water queues in common areas.

Why This Tool Exists

In many communities, managing water queues can be a challenge, as it runs on a rotating schedule rather than 24/7. Missing an allocation day may cause critical water shortages that will last for a week on households.

Project goals

This utility provides an accesible public portals to monitor local nieghbourhood water status updates.
