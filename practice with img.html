My little backyard sanctuary

Welcome to my happy place!

Honestly i never thought i would have such a peaceful spot in my own backyard. which is really nice because i wanted a place to relax and unwind.

There is nothing quite like sitting out here with a great book, a warm cup of tea, and just letting the stress melt away surrounded by all this quiet greean space. here is a quick look at what is currently thriving in my mini garden oasis:

The green herbs

  • Sweet basil:The plant that started it all. it smells incredible and saves me so much money on pizza nights.
  • Pepperiment:This one grows like absolute crazy, but it makes the most refreshing iced tea you will ever taste.

My favorite veggies

  1. Cherry tomatoes::watching them ripen and turn a deep red is honestly the best part of my morning routine.
  2. Jalapeño peppers:perfect for adding a little bit of unexpected kick to my favorite dishes
  3. Baby spinach: a bit dramatic when it comes to watering, but it bounces back after a quick watering.
